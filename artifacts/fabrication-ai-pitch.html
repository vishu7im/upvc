The opportunity

The engine is fast. Getting data into it is not.

Fabricators still spend valuable estimator time reading drawings, identifying openings, entering dimensions, checking prices and preparing customer documents.

01

Manual takeoff

Plans and enquiries arrive as PDFs, photos, notes and spreadsheets. Every opening must be interpreted and entered.

02

Slow response

Repetitive data entry delays quotes and reduces the number of opportunities a team can process.

03

Commercial risk

Missed openings, inconsistent selections and incomplete prices can reduce margin or create production rework.

The premium value is not “having AI.” It is moving from enquiry to an accurate draft order with less manual effort.
